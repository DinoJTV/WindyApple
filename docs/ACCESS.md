## Before you buy

The product page must confirm:

- Supported Windows versions and processor architectures.
- Supported iPad models and iPadOS versions.
- Connection method and any network, cable, or companion-app requirements.
- Installation steps and required components.
- Price, included updates, and support terms.

These details have not yet been verified for the supplied application. This repository does not claim a specific protocol, latency, audio capability, or automatic update system.

## Download and installation

Once the product is available, purchase through the official Gumroad product page and open the download link in your receipt. You can also use your Gumroad Library if the purchase is associated with your account.

Follow the instructions supplied with the purchased package. The exact installation and connection walkthrough will be added after it is checked against the app.

For unfamiliar or unexpected Windows security prompts, verify the download source and contact the seller rather than bypassing the warning.

## Updates

Read [CHANGELOG.md](../CHANGELOG.md) for announced changes. Download eligible updates through the same Gumroad purchase access, according to the product's stated update terms.
