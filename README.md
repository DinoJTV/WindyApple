# WindyApple

### Your iPad screen, on Windows.

WindyApple is an **iPad-to-Windows screen-sharing app**. Bring your iPad's screen to your Windows computer for presentations, demonstrations, and everyday sharing.

This is WindyApple's public product hub: setup guidance, release notes, and a place to report issues.

**[Get WindyApple](#purchase--download)** · **[Installation](#installation)** · **[Updates](#updates)** · **[Support](SUPPORT.md)**

## Purchase & download

> **Gumroad purchase/download link: coming soon.**
> The official product URL will be added here when it is ready.

WindyApple's paid Windows download will be delivered through Gumroad. The executable is not included in this repository or in public GitHub release assets.

Once the product is available:

1. Purchase WindyApple through the official Gumroad link above.
2. Open the download link in your Gumroad receipt, or access your purchase through your Gumroad Library.
3. Download the Windows package supplied with your purchase.

Cloning this repository or choosing GitHub's **Download ZIP** gives you the public documentation, not the application.

## Installation

1. Download WindyApple from your Gumroad purchase.
2. Follow the installation or launch instructions included with that download.
3. Open WindyApple on your Windows computer, then follow the supplied instructions to connect your iPad and begin sharing.

**Compatibility and connection details are awaiting confirmation.** Supported Windows and iPadOS versions, any required components, and the connection method will be documented before paid access launches.

See [Access & setup](docs/ACCESS.md) for download help and the information to check before buying.

## Updates

Public changes will be recorded in the [changelog](CHANGELOG.md). Paid application updates will be delivered through Gumroad under the terms stated on the product page.
